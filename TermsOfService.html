5. Pharmacy Verification Process

How pharmacies are reviewed and approved

All pharmacies that apply to join the MediFinder network go through a manual verification process before being approved and listed on the platform.

Verification Steps

  1. Pharmacy submits registration form with required business and personal information.
  2. Required documents are uploaded, including CNIC, Drug License, and Pharmacy Registration.
  3. MediFinder administrators review the submitted information and documents.
  4. Approval or rejection is communicated to the applicant via email.
  5. Approved pharmacies gain access to the full pharmacy dashboard and management tools.
MediFinder reserves the right to reject any pharmacy application that contains incomplete, inaccurate, or fraudulent information, or that does not meet our verification standards.

Submitting false or forged documents is a serious violation of these terms and may result in permanent account termination and reporting to relevant authorities.
