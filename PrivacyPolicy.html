1. Introduction

Our commitment to your privacy

MediFinder ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of all individuals who use our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and store it, and what rights you have over your data.

This policy applies to all users of MediFinder — including registered patients/users and registered pharmacists — when using our web platform or any related services.

By using MediFinder, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

3. User Information

Data collected from patients and registered users

When you create a user account on MediFinder, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name — to identify your account.
  • Email Address — for login, verification, and notifications.
  • Password — stored securely in hashed/encrypted form via Supabase Auth.
  • Phone Number — for account communication and support.
  • City — to show relevant nearby pharmacies.
  • Address — optional, to improve location-based search accuracy.
  • GPS Coordinates — collected with your permission to find the nearest pharmacies.

This information is used solely to provide you with our core services — including medicine search, pharmacy discovery, and medication reminders.

4. Pharmacist Information

Data collected during pharmacy registration

When a pharmacy registers on MediFinder, we collect the following business and personal information:

Personal & Business Details

  • Pharmacy Name and Owner/Manager Name
  • Phone Number and Email Address
  • CNIC Number
  • Drug License Number
  • Pharmacy Registration Number
  • Pharmacy Type and Operating Hours
  • Delivery Availability Status

Location Information

  • Province, City, Street Address, and Nearby Landmark
  • GPS Coordinates — to display your pharmacy on patient maps

Operational Data

  • Inventory records and product information
  • Billing records and transaction history
  • Sales data and demand forecasting information

This information is used to verify your pharmacy, display it in patient searches, and power the pharmacy management dashboard.

How Your Inventory Data Is Used in User Search

This is an important data usage that all registered pharmacists must be aware of:

  • Your pharmacy's live inventory data — including medicine names, availability status, and stock levels — is the data that directly powers the medicine search results that users see on MediFinder.
  • When a user searches for a specific medicine, MediFinder queries your registered inventory in real time and displays your pharmacy in the results if that medicine is marked as available in your stock.
  • Your pharmacy name, distance from the user, operating hours, and medicine availability are shown to users in search results.
  • If your inventory is outdated or incorrect, users may be misled about what is actually available at your pharmacy.

By maintaining your inventory on MediFinder, you consent to this data being actively used to serve real-time search results to users. Keeping your inventory accurate is both your responsibility and essential to the platform's core function.

5. Uploaded Documents

How we handle sensitive verification files

During pharmacy registration, you may be required to upload the following documents for verification purposes:

  • CNIC (National Identity Card) document
  • Pharmacy Registration Certificate
  • Drug License Document
These documents are stored securely using Supabase Storage and are accessed only by MediFinder administrators for the purpose of verifying your pharmacy registration.

Uploaded documents are:

  • Not shared with users or other pharmacies under any circumstance.
  • Stored with access controls that restrict viewing to authorized administrators only.
  • Retained for as long as your pharmacy account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as required for legal and compliance purposes.

6. Location Data Usage

How and why we use your location

Location data is a core part of how MediFinder works. We use your location to help you find pharmacies near you and to display accurate distance information.

For Users

  • Your GPS coordinates are collected with your explicit permission when you click "Detect My Location" during signup or search.
  • Location data is used to calculate distances to nearby pharmacies and sort search results by proximity.
  • We do not track your location continuously or in the background.

For Pharmacies

  • Your pharmacy's GPS coordinates are used to display your location on the patient-facing map.
  • Location data is used to match your pharmacy with user searches in your geographic area.
Location access is requested through your browser and is optional. However, some features — such as "Find Nearest Pharmacy" — require location access to function properly.

7. Search History & Analytics

How we use search behavior data

MediFinder collects and analyzes aggregated search and usage data to improve the platform's performance and provide valuable insights to pharmacies.

What we collect

  • Medicines and pharmaceutical products you search for.
  • Pharmacies you view or interact with.
  • Search patterns and frequency of specific medicine queries.

How we use it

  • Improve search relevance — to surface more accurate and helpful results.
  • Demand forecasting — aggregated search trends help pharmacies understand which medicines are in high demand in their area.
  • Restocking suggestions — pharmacies receive data-driven suggestions about which medicines to stock.
  • Platform optimization — to identify and fix performance issues and improve the overall user experience.
Analytics and demand forecasting data shared with pharmacies is always aggregated and anonymized. Individual user search histories are never shared directly with any pharmacy or third party.

Pharmacy Inventory & User-Facing Search Results

How Pharmacy Stock Data Connects to User Searches

This section explains the direct relationship between a pharmacy's inventory and what users see when they search:

  • When a user searches for a medicine, MediFinder queries the live inventory records of registered pharmacies in real time to determine availability.
  • Pharmacies whose stock includes the searched medicine will appear in the user's search results, along with their name, location, distance, and availability status.
  • A pharmacy's stock data is therefore the foundation of the user-facing availability feature — this is the primary way pharmacy inventory data is used on this platform.
  • Aggregated, anonymized search demand data (e.g. which medicines are searched most in a city) is also made available to pharmacies as demand forecasting insights on their dashboard — this data never identifies individual users.

Pharmacists registering on MediFinder consent to their inventory being used in this way. This data usage is fundamental to the platform's purpose of connecting patients with nearby medicine availability.

9. How Data Is Stored and Protected

Our security practices and infrastructure

We take the security of your data seriously. MediFinder uses the following practices to protect your information:

  • Encryption — all data is transmitted over HTTPS using TLS encryption. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Access Controls — database access is restricted to authorized team members and governed by role-based permissions.
  • Secure Authentication — user and pharmacy authentication is handled through Supabase Auth, which includes protections against common attack vectors.
  • File Storage Security — uploaded documents are stored in Supabase Storage with access restricted to authorized administrators only.
  • Row-Level Security — Supabase's row-level security policies ensure that users and pharmacies can only access their own data.
While we implement strong security measures, no system is completely immune to threats. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login credentials confidential.

11. Data Retention

How long we keep your information

We retain your data only for as long as it is necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations:

  • Active Accounts — your data is retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • Deleted Accounts — when you delete your account, your personal information is removed from our active databases within a reasonable time. Some anonymized or aggregated data may be retained for analytics purposes.
  • Pharmacy Documents — verification documents are retained while your pharmacy account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter for legal and compliance purposes.
  • Transaction and Billing Records — may be retained for longer periods as required by applicable financial regulations.
  • Search History — aggregated and anonymized search data may be retained indefinitely for platform improvement and analytics purposes.

If you would like to request deletion of your data, please contact us using the details in the Contact section below.
